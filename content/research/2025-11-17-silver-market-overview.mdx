# Silver Market Overview - November 2025

This report provides an overview of the global silver market, including supply dynamics, demand drivers, and key trends shaping the industry.

## Executive Summary

Silver occupies a unique position as both a precious metal and an industrial commodity. Its dual nature creates complex market dynamics driven by:

- **Industrial demand** (~50% of total demand)
- **Investment demand** (~25% of total demand)
- **Jewelry and silverware** (~20% of total demand)
- **Photography and other** (~5% of total demand)

<Callout type="note">
**Data Sources:** This analysis draws from USGS, Silver Institute, World Silver Survey, and LBMA data. All figures are estimates and subject to revision.
</Callout>

## Supply Analysis

### Global Mine Production

Annual silver mine production has remained relatively stable in recent years:

| Year | Production (Million oz) | Change (%) |
|------|------------------------|------------|
| 2022 | 822.4                  | +0.5%      |
| 2023 | 830.5                  | +1.0%      |
| 2024 | 838.2*                 | +0.9%      |

*Estimate

**Key Producing Countries (2024):**

1. **Mexico** - 189M oz (22.5% of global)
2. **Peru** - 110M oz (13.1%)
3. **China** - 103M oz (12.3%)
4. **Australia** - 48M oz (5.7%)
5. **Russia** - 45M oz (5.4%)

### Primary vs. By-Product Production

A critical factor in silver supply: approximately **70% of silver is mined as a by-product** of base metals (copper, lead, zinc) and gold mining.

This has important implications:

- Silver production is partly dependent on base metal prices
- Supply is relatively inelastic to silver price changes in the short term
- Mine closures in base metals can impact silver supply

### Recycling

Silver recycling contributes significantly to total supply:

- **2024 Recycling:** ~180M oz (21% of total supply)
- **Sources:** Industrial scrap, jewelry, photography, coins

## Demand Analysis

### Industrial Demand

Industrial applications account for the largest share of silver demand:

**Key Industrial Uses:**

1. **Electronics & Electrical** (~240M oz/year)
   - Switches and contacts
   - Printed circuit boards
   - RFID chips
   - Membrane switches

2. **Solar Panels** (~130M oz/year)
   - Photovoltaic cells
   - Growing rapidly with renewable energy adoption
   - Expected to increase 15-20% annually

3. **Automotive** (~60M oz/year)
   - Electrical systems
   - Electric vehicle components
   - Expected growth with EV adoption

4. **Medical & Healthcare** (~50M oz/year)
   - Antimicrobial applications
   - Medical devices
   - Bandages and wound care

### Investment Demand

Physical investment in silver includes:

- **Coins & Bars** (~200M oz/year)
- **ETFs** - Net flows vary significantly by year
- **Silver-backed cryptocurrencies** - Emerging sector

**Factors Driving Investment Demand:**

- Inflation hedging
- Currency devaluation concerns
- Portfolio diversification
- Store of value

### Jewelry and Silverware

Traditional demand from jewelry and silverware:

- **2024 Demand:** ~170M oz
- **Key Markets:** India, United States, Thailand, Italy
- **Trends:** Declining in developed markets, stable in emerging markets

## Market Dynamics

### Price Drivers

Silver prices are influenced by multiple factors:

1. **Industrial demand** - Economic growth correlates with industrial usage
2. **Investment flows** - Safe haven demand during uncertainty
3. **Gold/Silver ratio** - Historical relationship influences relative value
4. **Currency movements** - Particularly USD strength/weakness
5. **Supply constraints** - Mine closures, production disruptions

### Gold/Silver Ratio

The gold/silver ratio has historically ranged between 40:1 and 80:1:

- **Current Ratio:** ~75:1 (as of November 2025)
- **Historical Average:** ~60:1
- **Implications:** Some analysts view ratios >75 as indicating undervalued silver

### Future Trends

Several trends are likely to shape the silver market:

**Bullish Factors:**

- Growing solar panel adoption
- Electric vehicle proliferation
- 5G and IoT expansion
- Green energy transition
- Potential supply constraints

**Bearish Factors:**

- Recycling efficiency improvements
- Technological substitution (in some applications)
- Economic slowdown reducing industrial demand
- Strength in competing assets

## Data Gaps and Limitations

Transparency about what we know and don't know:

- **Above-ground stocks** - No reliable global inventory data
- **Central bank holdings** - Limited disclosure from some countries
- **Private investment** - Difficult to track accurately
- **Scrap flows** - Estimates vary widely

## Conclusion

The silver market presents a complex interplay of industrial, investment, and traditional demand against a relatively constrained supply base. The metal's unique position as both an industrial commodity and monetary metal creates distinct opportunities and risks.

Key takeaways:

1. Supply is largely inelastic in the short term
2. Industrial demand is growing, particularly in green technologies
3. Investment demand can be highly volatile
4. Long-term fundamentals appear supportive
5. Significant data gaps remain in global inventory tracking
