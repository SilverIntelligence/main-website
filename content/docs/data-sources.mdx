# Data Sources

Transparency is at the core of Wall Street Silver. This document outlines our data sources, collection methodology, and quality assurance processes.

## Primary Sources

### 1. London Bullion Market Association (LBMA)

The LBMA provides authoritative pricing data for gold and silver through twice-daily fixings.

- **Data Type:** Spot prices, fixing prices
- **Update Frequency:** Daily (AM/PM fixes)
- **Historical Coverage:** 1968-present
- **Attribution:** All LBMA data is clearly marked and timestamped

### 2. Commodities Exchanges

We aggregate data from major global exchanges:

- **COMEX** (CME Group) - Futures and options data
- **Shanghai Gold Exchange** - Asian market pricing
- **Other regional exchanges** - For comprehensive coverage

### 3. Government Statistics

Official production and consumption data from:

- U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)
- World Silver Survey
- National mining agencies

### 4. Industry Reports

Curated data from reputable industry sources:

- Silver Institute annual surveys
- CPM Group research
- Metals Focus reports

## Data Collection Methodology

### Automated Ingestion

Our data pipeline automatically collects and processes data from public APIs and feeds:

```typescript
// Example data ingestion flow (simplified)
const pipeline = {
```

### Quality Assurance

Every data point goes through multiple validation steps:

1. **Schema Validation** - Ensure data matches expected format
2. **Range Checks** - Flag outliers and anomalies
3. **Cross-Source Verification** - Compare across multiple sources
4. **Manual Review** - Human oversight for critical data

<Callout type="warning">
**Data Lag:** Real-time data may have a 1-5 minute delay depending on the source. Historical data is updated daily.
</Callout>

## Data Attribution

All data in our platform includes:

- **Source Name** - Where the data originated
- **Timestamp** - When it was collected
- **Version** - Data revision history
- **License** - Usage rights and restrictions

Example metadata:

```json
{
  "value": 24.85,
  "unit": "USD/oz",
  "metal": "silver",
  "timestamp": "2025-11-17T14:00:00Z",
  "source": {
    "name": "LBMA",
    "type": "official_fixing",
    "license": "public_data"
  },
  "version": "1.0"
}
```

## Data Limitations

We believe in transparency about what our data can and cannot tell you:

- **Market Coverage:** We focus on major markets; some regional exchanges may not be included
- **Historical Gaps:** Some historical data has known gaps or quality issues
- **Estimates:** Certain data points (e.g., recycling rates) are estimates based on industry models

## Using Our Data

### Terms of Use

Data accessed through our platform is subject to:

1. Original source licenses
2. Our platform's [Terms of Service](/legal/terms)
3. Proper attribution requirements

### Best Practices

When using our data:

- **Always cite sources** - Include both Wall Street Silver and the original source
- **Check timestamps** - Ensure data freshness matches your needs
- **Understand context** - Read methodology notes for each dataset
- **Report issues** - Help us improve by reporting data quality concerns
