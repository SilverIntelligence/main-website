# Welcome to Wall Street Silver Developer Hub

We're excited to announce the launch of the Wall Street Silver Developer Hub—a new platform dedicated to making precious metals market data and analytics accessible to everyone.

## Why We Built This

For too long, high-quality market data and research tools have been locked behind expensive paywalls, accessible only to large institutions. We believe that shouldn't be the case.

Wall Street Silver is building **open infrastructure for precious metals analytics**. Our goal is to provide developers, researchers, and analysts with the tools they need to understand and analyze silver and precious metals markets.

## What We're Building

### 1. Open APIs

We're developing REST and GraphQL APIs that provide:

- Real-time and historical price data
- Market statistics and indicators
- Cross-market analysis
- Supply and demand metrics

**Status:** Alpha release planned for Q1 2025. [Sign up for early access](/contact).

### 2. Research Database

A curated collection of market research, analysis, and data visualizations—all with proper citations and methodology notes.

**Status:** Live now! Browse our [research section](/research).

### 3. Developer Tools

SDKs, libraries, and integration examples to make it easy to build on our platform.

**Status:** TypeScript SDK in development. Python and Go SDKs planned for Q2 2025.

### 4. Community Resources

Forums, discussions, and collaborative tools for the precious metals analytics community.

**Status:** GitHub Discussions open now.

## Our Principles

### Transparency

All our data includes source attribution and methodology notes. No black boxes.

### Open Source

Our tools, libraries, and documentation are open source. Contribute on [GitHub](https://github.com/wallstreetsilver).

### Quality

We prioritize data quality and accuracy over quantity. Every data point is validated.

### Community

We're building this together. Your feedback shapes our roadmap.
